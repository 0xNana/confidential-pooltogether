# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want Confidential PoolTogether to be a welcoming, respectful, and productive project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical knowledge.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Give actionable feedback and accept correction gracefully.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, and responsible-disclosure requests.
- Help keep issues, pull requests, and community spaces focused and useful.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, sustained disruption, or deliberately misleading claims.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of wallet keys, credentials, or user funds.

## Scope

This code applies in project repositories, issue trackers, reviews, discussions, and public spaces where someone is representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `hellodaries@gmail.com`. Include relevant links or screenshots and enough context to evaluate the incident. Do not disclose sensitive personal information beyond what is necessary.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful, unsafe, or inconsistent with this code. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical.
